About

Yeyen M

I’m a web and graphic designer from Pati, Indonesia. I help small businesses and personal brands get a clean, modern look online — landing pages, simple business sites, product catalogs, and basic brand kits.

I start with a short plan, a fixed price, and a deadline. You get updates as the work moves, and revisions until the pages feel right. I work more than 30 hours a week and reply during business hours in the Jakarta time zone.

The two concept sites in this portfolio (Arunika and Kopi Lumintu) are original demonstration pieces. They are not commissioned client projects, and they do not use real testimonials, sales figures, or review scores.
