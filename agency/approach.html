Approach

Fewer surprises. A plan you can hold before design starts.

Four steps, always in the same order.

1. Listen. A short call or written brief covers the offer, the audience, existing materials, and the date the pages need to be live.

2. Plan. You receive a page list, a timeline, and a fixed price. Work does not begin until that plan is agreed.

3. Design and build. Layouts are made with real or agreed sample copy, then built as responsive pages. You review at agreed checkpoints, not in a last-minute rush.

4. Launch and handoff. The site is prepared for publishing. You get a short note on what was built and how to request later changes.

This page is part of a concept demo. It describes a working method; it is not a service contract.
