Services

Clear offers, written the way a client would brief them.

01

Brand foundations

A compact kit: typography, color, a simple mark, and voice notes. Enough to keep a website, proposal, and social crop looking like the same company.

02

Marketing websites

Six to eight page sites are a typical brief: home, services, work, approach, about, and contact, with room for a short journal if the team writes.

03

Landing pages

Single-purpose pages for a campaign or service line, with a form or a clear next step. Designed mobile-first.

04

Content structure

Headings, page outlines, and sample copy so the site does not launch on placeholder text.

05

Responsive build

Static HTML, CSS, and light JavaScript that stay fast on modest connections and remain usable without a heavy framework.

06

Launch support

A checklist for metadata, favicons, form behavior, and a short handoff so the team knows what was built.

This service list is part of a concept demo. It describes the kind of work the portfolio is meant to show. It is not a live agency catalog with pricing packages.
