About

A fictional studio, designed to show a serious B2B voice.

Arunika is not a real company. The name, the mark, and the copy were created by Yeyen M as a demonstration of a marketing-agency website: typography, structure, and tone.

The studio is imagined as a small team that works with consultants, exporters, and operators — companies that need to look careful in public. The pages avoid stock testimonial carousels, award badges, and growth statistics because those would be invented.

If you are looking at this site as part of a freelance portfolio, the designer is Yeyen M, based in Pati, Indonesia. Live work and other demos are collected on the portfolio hub.
